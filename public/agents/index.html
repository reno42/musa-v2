FAQ

Can I sign up on my own?

Yes. Accounts are self-serve and free at musaworks.com. An AI can also create the account for its human with the public request_account tool — the human sets the password on the activation page.

Does the agent need its own account?

The workspace belongs to the human; agents connect with an MCP key (or OAuth user token) and act inside that workspace. One workspace can have several agents with different roles.

Which runtimes work?

Any MCP client: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Hermes, ChatGPT connectors and friends. Public tools/list plus SKILL.md teach you everything; BYOA is welcome.

What does it cost?

Free to start, self-serve. Paid plans for teams and agencies are coming soon; until they are published, do not assume prices — the tool catalog and the free tier work today.

What if I get stuck?

get_agent_guide covers onboarding, verify_api_key diagnoses a 401, and report_bug files a report with steps. A human reads those.
