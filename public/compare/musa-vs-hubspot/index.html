Musa vs HubSpot

CRM and marketing suite: email, landing pages, automation, ads, social (Pro) and reports, all on top of its CRM. Here's how it compares with Musa.

Updated: November 2026

Short answer

HubSpot Marketing Hub: CRM and marketing suite: email, landing pages, automation, ads, social (Pro) and reports, all on top of its CRM. Musa is a content platform that brings together design, video, publishing, a message inbox, ads, CRM, a client portal and AI agents for multiple brands. Choose HubSpot if your company sells B2B with long cycles and lives on email marketing and lead automation. Choose Musa if your business is content: producing, publishing and selling creative services for several brands.

Where Musa wins

  • Musa designs and edits graphic pieces and video; HubSpot doesn't.
  • Social calendar, publishing and inbox from the first plan.
  • Multiple brands with their own identity and a portal per client, no add-ons.
  • No onboarding fees and BYOK for AI.

Where HubSpot wins

  • Very mature CRM and email marketing automation.
  • A large ecosystem of integrations and certifications.
  • Advanced attribution reporting.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Musa and HubSpot?

HubSpot Marketing Hub: CRM and marketing suite: email, landing pages, automation, ads, social (Pro) and reports, all on top of its CRM. Musa brings design, video, calendar and publishing, inbox, ads, CRM, client portal and AI agents via MCP into one platform for multiple brands.

Is Musa an alternative to HubSpot?

Yes, if your business is content: producing, publishing and selling creative services for several brands. HubSpot is still the better option if your company sells B2B with long cycles and lives on email marketing and lead automation.

How much does HubSpot cost?

According to its public information reviewed in November 2026: Starter US$20 per seat/mo · Professional ~US$890/mo (3 seats) + US$3,000 onboarding · Enterprise ~US$3,600/mo. Check the current price on its website.

Can I use Musa and HubSpot together?

Many companies use HubSpot as their corporate CRM and Musa to produce each area's content.

What does Musa have that HubSpot doesn't?

According to HubSpot's public information, it doesn't include: photo editor, motion and animation, bulk production, brand manual and validation, aI clones, takes with teleprompter, growth loops, bring your own AI key (BYOK). Musa includes all of that.
