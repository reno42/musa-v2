## Musa vs Canva

https://design.futuraria.com/compare/musa-vs-canva/

- Canva: Massive design editor with templates, AI (Canva AI 2.0), video, docs and ads with Canva Grow.
- Price of Canva: Free · Pro US$18/mo · Business US$25 per person/mo · Enterprise custom
- Choose Canva if: If you only need to design one-off pieces fast and don't manage clients, sales or ads from the same tool.
- Choose Musa if: If you're a freelancer, agency or marketing team and want design, publishing, conversations, ads and sales to live in one place per brand.
- Together: Many teams keep using Canva for quick pieces; Musa imports HTML and SVG designs so you can work them with your brand templates.

## Musa vs Adobe

https://design.futuraria.com/compare/musa-vs-adobe/

- Adobe Express y Firefly: Adobe's content suite: Express for quick pieces, Firefly for generative AI and GenStudio for large enterprises.
- Price of Adobe: Express Premium US$9.99/mo · Firefly from US$19.99/mo · GenStudio custom

## FAQ

**What is Musa?** Musa, by Futuraria, is a content platform for freelancers, agencies and companies. It brings into one place what is usually split across Canva, Hootsuite, Meta Ads Manager and a CRM: planning, design, video editing, publishing, replying to messages, running ads, selling and measuring for multiple brands, with your team and your AI agents in the same space.

**What's the best Canva alternative for agencies?** For agencies that manage several brands, Musa is a Canva alternative that also includes a client portal, calendar and publishing, a message inbox, ad campaigns and a CRM with quotes. Canva is still better if you only need to design one-off pieces with a huge template library.

**Does Musa replace Hootsuite, Metricool or Sprout Social?** For most freelancers and agencies, yes: Musa schedules and publishes to social networks, has a unified inbox and reports per brand. It also designs the content, which those tools don't. If you need enterprise-grade social listening, Sprout or Hootsuite are still more complete at that.

**What does BYOK mean in Musa?** BYOK (bring your own key) means you connect your own AI accounts, such as OpenAI, Gemini or Runway, and pay for generation directly to those providers, at cost. If you'd rather not set anything up, you can also use Musa's built-in AI.

**What is MCP and why does it matter?** MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard assistants like Claude, ChatGPT or Codex use to operate external tools. Musa exposes 280+ tools via MCP: your agent can design, adapt formats, schedule, publish, reply and create quotes inside Musa, with the permissions you give it.

**Does Musa work for companies with several departments?** Yes. Corporate spaces have internal areas (HR, Innovation, Product, Marketing, Sales…), Marketing approval flows, invited external agencies and locked brand templates.

**Can I give my clients access?** Yes. Each client can have their own portal: they only see their brand, approve pieces, and review briefs, quotes and the calendar.

**Can I start for free?** Yes, you can create a free account and try Musa with your brand. If you already used Musa, sign in with the same account.
