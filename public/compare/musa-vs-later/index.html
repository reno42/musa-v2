A visual scheduler for Instagram, TikTok and other networks, with link in bio and influencer marketing (Later Influence). Here's how it compares with Musa.

Short answer

Later: A visual scheduler for Instagram, TikTok and other networks, with link in bio and influencer marketing (Later Influence). Musa is a content platform that brings together design, video, publishing, a message inbox, ads, CRM, a client portal and AI agents for multiple brands. Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Musa and Later?

Later: A visual scheduler for Instagram, TikTok and other networks, with link in bio and influencer marketing (Later Influence). Musa brings design, video, calendar and publishing, inbox, ads, CRM, client portal and AI agents via MCP into one platform for multiple brands.

Is Musa an alternative to Later?

Yes, if you manage the full content of several brands. Later is still the better option if your focus is Instagram with influencers and link in bio.

How much does Later cost?

According to its public information reviewed in November 2026: Starter ~US$25 · Growth ~US$45 · Advanced ~US$80 per month. Check the current price on its website.

Can I use Musa and Later together?

Produce in Musa and, if your influencer program lives in Later, keep it there.

What does Musa have that Later doesn't?

According to Later's public information, it doesn't include: design editor, photo editor, video editor, motion and animation, visual and code in the same file, bulk production, brand manual and validation, events, forms, email marketing, aI clones, takes with teleprompter, ad campaigns, cRM and sales funnel, quotes with client signature, funnel simulator, growth loops, client intranet, aI agents that operate the platform (MCP), bring your own AI key (BYOK), automations. Musa includes all of that.
