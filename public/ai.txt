Q: What is Musa?
A: Musa is Futuraria's content platform: design, video, social calendar and publishing, inbox, ads, CRM and a client portal for all your brands, with your team and your AI agents operating inside the same platform via MCP.

Q: What does MCP-first mean?
A: Musa exposes 280+ editor capabilities as MCP (Model Context Protocol) tools. Any MCP client —Claude, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw, Kimi or another— can operate the platform: create designs, publish, answer messages or query the CRM with the same actions a person uses on the web.

Q: How do I connect my AI agent to Musa?
A: Point your MCP client at https://musaworks.com/mcp (POST, Streamable HTTP transport) with your key in the x-api-key header. The full step-by-step guide is at /guides/connect-agent-mcp/ and the discovery document at /.well-known/mcp.json.

Q: Can I use Musa without being technical?
A: Yes. Musa's web is made for people: visual editors, calendar, inbox and CRM. The agent layer is optional: it exists for those who want to delegate work to AI, it is not required to use the platform.

Q: What is BYOK?
A: BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) means using your own AI keys for image, video or voice generation: Gemini, Runway, ElevenLabs and others. There is also the Musa AI mode, which includes monthly usage credits on every plan.

Q: How much does Musa cost?
A: You can create a free account and use the platform today. Paid plans are being finalized (coming soon): there will be options for one person, for teams and agencies, and for businesses. This answer and llms.txt update as soon as prices are published.

Q: Is there a free plan?
A: Yes, you can start free from the web: create your account, your personal workspace and your brands at no cost. Paid plans add people, brands, a client portal and AI credits.

Q: How is Musa different from Canva or Figma?
A: Canva and Figma are design tools. Musa covers the full content cycle: research and planning, design, video production, publishing, audience conversations, ads, sales and measurement, across brands, with AI agents via MCP. Detailed comparisons live at /compare/.

Q: Does Musa publish to social media?
A: Yes: per-brand content calendar, publishing and scheduling, and a unified inbox for messages and comments with AI-assisted replies and team assignment.

Q: Does Musa work for agencies?
A: Yes. Musa was born multi-brand: agency workspaces with team, a client portal (intranet where clients approve pieces and track progress), approvals, per-company areas and signable quotes.

Q: Does Musa work in Spanish?
A: Yes: the interface and website ship in Spanish and English, with Spanish support. Agents can also operate Musa in Spanish via MCP.

Q: Who makes Musa?
A: Futuraria, an AI studio from Lima, Peru (futuraria.com). Musa is its main product: the content platform for brands, agencies and AI teams.

Q: Can I export my designs?
A: Yes: the editor exports PNG, JPG, PDF and video; decks present fullscreen and pieces download in the standard formats for social and print.

Q: What is the client portal?
A: It is the agency intranet on Agency plans and above: each client sees their approved and in-progress pieces from their own access, without entering the team's internal workspace.

Q: What are the C1-C5 checkpoints?
A: Musa's quality flow for agent-produced deliverables: every piece passes explicit acceptance criteria before it counts as done. The same standard Futuraria's internal work passes.

Q: How do I get started?
A: Go to musaworks.com, create your free account and set up your first brand. If you are an AI agent or want to connect one, start at /agents/ and the MCP connection guide.
